ThinkUplift — Ideas & Roadmap
Ye file soch ka bank hai, promise nahi. Jo khayal aaye, yahan likh do.
Har idea ke saamne ek hi sawaal rakhna hai:
"Kya isse insaan wiser, calmer, stronger, kinder banega?"
Jawab NAHI ho to wo feature nahi banega — chahe kitna hi aakarshak lage.
Mission: Read · Reflect · Rebuild
Success ka paimana: kitni kahaniyan poori padhi gayin, kitne reflections likhe gaye — screen time kabhi nahi.

Splash, Home, Story Reader, Reflection Journal, Profile
Dark mode, font size, bookmark, reading streak

Story Studio (studio.html) — form bharo, file khud ban jaati hai
Sab data phone par (no login, no cloud, no ads, no social features)

🤝 Phase 3 — Mentor Directory (sirf parichay, ilaaj nahi)
Model: Aap sampark karwate ho. Salah, fees, zimmedari — professional aur user ke beech.
Shartein (inme koi chhoot nahi):
Verification — doctor ka registration number (NMC/State Council) khud jaancho
Likhit sehmati — naam/number app par dene ki permission likhit mein
Commission bilkul nahi — India mein referral par paisa lena niyamon ke khilaf hai;
coach/motivator ke saath bhi nahi. Ye seva rahe, dhanda nahi
Saaf disclaimer app ke Terms mein
Shuruaat: 2–3 log jinhe aap khud jaante ho. 50 ki list mat banao.
Order (risk ke hisab se):
Pehle: career · business · financial · padhai (risk kam)
Sabse aakhir: mental health (sabse zyada jaanch ke saath)
Crisis ke liye hamesha 24x7 helpline — kisi ek insaan ka personal number kabhi nahi

💰 Business Model (philosophy ke saath jo chalte hain)
Sansthaon ko licence ⭐ (sabse bada aur sabse kam socha gaya raasta)
Schools, colleges, de-addiction centres, jails, NGOs, HR wellness programs
Unhe aisi hi calm, non-addictive cheez chahiye
Chhapi hui kitab — 100 kahaniyan = ek kitab. App muft, kitab bikegi.
Aap roz likh hi rahe ho — bas pata nahi.
Patron/support model — sabke liye muft, jo chahe swechha se de (Wikipedia ka tarika)
Affiliate (kitabon par commission) — kaam karta hai, par chetavni: dimaag chupke se
"jo zyada deti hai" wo suggest karne lagega. Karo to saaf likho ki commission milta hai
Kabhi nahi: ads · premium-locked kahaniyan (nirash insaan ke aage paywall = mission ke khilaf) ·
streak par guilt · engagement ke peeche bhagna
